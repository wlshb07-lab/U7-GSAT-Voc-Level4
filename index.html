📐 不只會考試：句子優化ing

把平淡無奇版句子升級成學測級句子。先看示範，再完成 10 題練習。

示範 Example
🍼 平淡無奇版：Larry has a job interview. He worries a lot. He thinks about how he will do.
🎓 學測版：Larry has a lot of anxiety over his job interview because he is worried about how he will perform.
升級點：worries a lot → a lot of anxiety over（名詞化，密度更高）；三句併一句，用 because 交代因果；how he will perform 以名詞子句取代鬆散的 thinks about。
